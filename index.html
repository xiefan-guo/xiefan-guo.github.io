About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Beihang University (BUAA), advised by Prof. Di Huang in the Intelligent Recognition and Image Processing (IRIP) Lab. My research interests lie in generative AI (GenAI) and multimodal agents.

Prior to my Ph.D., I received my M.S. from Beihang University in 2023 and my B.E. from Tianjin University in 2020. I have interned at Tencent Hunyuan, Shanghai AI Laboratory, Alibaba TongYi Lab, and Alibaba DAMO Academy.
